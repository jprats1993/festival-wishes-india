# Editorial Style

## Voice

Warm, concise, sincere, family-friendly, and easy to forward. Prefer emotionally specific wishes over generic filler.

## Wish format

Each wish should normally be 1–3 sentences and should identify an audience, relationship, or mood where useful.

Good categories include:

- brother, sister, parents, spouse, friends, colleagues;
- emotional, funny, respectful, short, formal, devotional;
- WhatsApp status, caption, card text, and long message.

## Titles and metadata

- Use descriptive titles that match the page's actual language and audience.
- Do not repeat the same festival keyword in every heading.
- Meta descriptions should explain what the visitor will find and why it is useful.
- Use one primary language per page; provide links to alternate language pages.

## Hindi conventions

- Prefer natural contemporary Hindi over unnecessarily Sanskritised wording.
- Use consistent punctuation and danda usage where appropriate.
- Preserve respectful forms such as “आप” when the audience is formal or elder family members.

## Hinglish conventions

- Use common Roman spellings that users actually type.
- Avoid forcing English words into every sentence.
- Keep spelling consistent within a page.

## Images

- Text on an image must match the approved text exactly.
- Do not place important text close to crop boundaries.
- Use high contrast and readable Devanagari fonts.
- Provide square, portrait, and social-preview variants only when they materially help sharing.
