# Content Policy

## Purpose

Publish useful, original, culturally respectful festival wishes and shareable greeting cards in Hindi, English, and Hinglish.

## Editorial rules

- Drafts are AI-assisted. Every new festival seed batch requires explicit owner approval before publication; routine expansions within an approved template may be auto-published after reviewer-agent audit.
- Write natural, complete sentences. Do not publish keyword-stuffed lists or synonym variations of the same wish.
- Every page must provide value beyond a collection of duplicated cards: add a useful introduction, context, or sharing guidance.
- Do not copy poems, quotes, lyrics, articles, or social-media forwards unless the owner has permission or the material is clearly public domain.
- Do not scrape Pinterest, Google Images, or other greeting sites.
- Use only original, licensed, or generated artwork and retain an asset record.
- Avoid political persuasion, sectarian claims, medical/financial advice, stereotypes, and claims about religious practice that have not been verified.
- Do not invent festival dates, regional customs, historical claims, or rituals. Flag uncertain claims for review.

## Language rules

- Hindi pages use Devanagari as the primary content language.
- English pages use English as the primary content language.
- Hinglish pages use readable Roman-script Hindi; do not mix scripts inside a single sentence unless intentional.
- Preserve meaning rather than translating word-for-word.
- An independent reviewer-agent audits every batch for duplication, unnatural Hindi/Hinglish, and cultural accuracy. Human review is preferred but not guaranteed; when no human reviewer is available, Hindi quality is treated as a declared experimental risk and monitored via engagement signals and owner spot-checks.

## AI transparency

AI may assist with drafting, translation, image ideation, and quality checks. The site must not claim that AI-generated text or imagery is human-authored. Add an editorial note where a visitor would reasonably expect to know how the content was produced.

## Publication gate

Reject or hold content when it has any of the following:

- near-duplicate wording;
- unnatural Hindi or transliteration;
- unsupported cultural or factual claims;
- unclear copyright provenance;
- missing alt text or asset metadata;
- no meaningful purpose beyond targeting a keyword.
