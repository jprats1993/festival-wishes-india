## Review requirements

- A reviewer-agent audits every batch. The first seed batch of a new festival requires explicit owner approval before publication.
- Regional ritual or historical claims require explicit verification.
- The agent may not invent dates or present uncertain customs as universal.
- The agent may expand a collection only after checking duplication and Search Console demand.
